Top to Bottom Narrative
1. Hero
One interface for the coding agents you use now and the ones you add later.

2. Pain Strip

Agent Sprawl

Too many CLIs, tabs, and copy-pasted context.

Workflow Stalls

SPUR is the control plane between your work and your agents: route by fit and cost, run workers in isolated worktrees, review before merge, and keep everything visible in one TUI.

For Team Leads
  • Standardize a mixed-agent stack behind one interface
  • See costs, activity, and handoffs in one place
  • Reduce vendor lock-in without losing control
