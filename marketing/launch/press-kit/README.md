## What's in this kit

- `one-pager.md` — single-page summary you can lift wholesale
- `logo-spec.md` — placeholder logo spec (no artwork shipped yet — see file for what to ask the designer)
- `screenshots-list.md` — the 8 screenshots we will provide on request, with framing notes
- `quotes.md` — founder soundbites, pre-cleared for verbatim use
- `faq-press.md` — 10 questions we expect and how we answer them honestly

## What's NOT in this kit

- Customer logos or testimonials. SPUR is pre-launch; we do not have named-user references to share yet, and we will not invent them. Ask again after Day 60.
- Funding figures. See FAQ.
- Roadmap dates beyond the next public milestone. We don't ship roadmaps we can't keep.
- Live demo link. Email us — we'll set up a 20-minute walkthrough.

## Embargo policy

If a story is timed to a specific launch beat (Product Hunt day, a co-marketing post, a feature reveal), we'll mark the embargo time in the email thread and provide assets 48 hours ahead. Otherwise everything in this kit is free to publish today.

For anything not in the kit (custom screenshots, exec headshots, deeper technical detail, a quote on a specific topic): ask. We respond within 24 hours on weekdays.
