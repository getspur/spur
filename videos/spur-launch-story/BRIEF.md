# SPUR launch story

## Intent

Create a 69-second landscape launch film in which a human works directly with one brain
agent. SPUR turns the brain's intent into Plan, Loop, and Ad hoc work, routes it to the
right workers, exposes progress in a control-tower view, returns concrete evidence, and
lets the brain review first. Only critical decisions escalate to the human.

## Assets

- Existing SPUR launch master and its music bed.
- Real product media shots from the launch package.
- Existing HyperFrames brain-control-flow insert and its design system.
- Approved production brief and product framing.

## Customizations

- Existing music with restrained transition SFX; no narration.
- Real TUI surfaces and human-readable evidence, not abstract AI imagery.
- Evidence-token motion for diffs, tests, traces, approvals, and escalation.
- A durable-context beat that proves work can resume without losing the thread.

## Notes

- Warm terminal documentary: dark graphite, warm ivory, amber control, green success,
  muted terracotta for critical escalation.
- Anti-AI-slop: no neon gradients, glitch language, floating brains, robots, or generic
  particle networks.
- No external media generation.
- Designed open and close; the extra nine seconds beyond the existing master are used
  for resume proof and calmer evidence holds, never filler.
