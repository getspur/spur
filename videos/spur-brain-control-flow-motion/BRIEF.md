# SPUR Brain Control Flow

## Intent

Create a concise animated visualization for the SPUR launch video. The brain is
the central operator-facing interface. It turns intent into Plan, Loop, or Ad
hoc work, delegates bounded tasks to specialist workers, receives evidence,
and reviews routine completions. Only a critical decision travels onward to
the human.

The graphic should read as a real operating model, not a futuristic AI network.
Use explicit labels, terminal geometry, evidence trails, and calm editorial
motion.

## Assets

No external assets. The insert is authored entirely with HTML, CSS, inline SVG,
and a deterministic GSAP timeline.

## Customizations

- Warm terminal documentary palette
- Graphite canvas, paper-white type, amber brain accent
- Green routine-review path, muted terracotta critical path
- Center-out expansion for worker delegation
- Drawn SVG lineage paths for dispatch and evidence return
- Restrained bloom behind the brain; no neon, glass, or generic AI imagery
